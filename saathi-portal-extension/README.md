# Saathi AI — Cybercrime Portal Auto-Filer (Chrome Extension)

This extension automatically pre-fills your verified cybercrime incident particulars directly into the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (**`cybercrime.gov.in`** and **`cybervolunteer.mha.gov.in`**).

## How to Install (Takes 30 Seconds):
1. Open Google Chrome or Brave browser.
2. In the URL bar, go to `chrome://extensions/`.
3. In the top-right corner, toggle **Developer mode** to ON.
4. Click **Load unpacked** (top-left).
5. Select this folder:
   `/Users/sumitsinha/Documents/Saathi AI/saathi-portal-extension`
6. The Saathi AI extension icon 🛡️ will appear in your browser toolbar!

## How to Use:
1. Confirm your complaint on Saathi AI ([http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000)).
2. Open `https://cybercrime.gov.in/login-terms-conditions`.
3. The extension automatically detects portal fields and injects your verified Disputed Amount, Suspect Details, Bank/Intermediary, and Narrative!
4. Solve the official live CAPTCHA and enter your statutory mobile SMS OTP to legally lodge your complaint.
