Next Steps: Protect Yourself & Report Financial Cyber Fraud

🏦

1. Notify Your Bank Immediately

Under RBI Zero-Liability Guidelines, immediately call your bank's 24x7 fraud customer care to block the debit card/UPI handle and request an immediate hold on the beneficiary account.

💡 Golden Hour: Report within 2-3 hours for highest recovery chance.
📞

2. Call Cybercrime Helpline 1930

Dial 1930 (formerly 155260), India's national citizen financial cyber fraud reporting helpline managed by the Ministry of Home Affairs (I4C).

📞 Call 1930 Now Toll-free across all Indian telecom operators
🌐

3. File on cybercrime.gov.in

Submit your formal digital complaint on the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. Prepare your transaction SMS, UPI IDs, and bank statement.
