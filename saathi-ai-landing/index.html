How You Interact With Saathi AI

From the moment unauthorized debit alerts arrive to submitting formal disputes, Saathi AI accompanies you through every step.

1

Send a Voice Note or Message

WhatsApp Input

Simply message the official Saathi AI WhatsApp number. Speak or type what happened: when money was debited, what app you used, and any links, phone numbers, or QR codes involved.

Voice notes are transcribed locally & automatically erased immediately.
2

AI Structures Your Incident Details

Local Intelligence

The local AI engine parses the raw audio transcript into standard cybercrime fields: transaction reference numbers, beneficiary bank accounts, scam modus operandi, and dispute amounts.

3

Review & Explicit Confirmation

Consent Gate

Saathi AI responds on WhatsApp with a clear summary and interactive buttons: Confirm, Edit, or Cancel. Zero reporting action is ever executed without your explicit tap.

4

Instant Relief Documents & Next Steps

Action Phase

Upon confirmation, you receive a pre-formatted Bank Dispute Email, an official Incident PDF, one-tap connection to 1930 Cyber Helpline, and a tracking timeline to monitor progress.

Security Guardrails

Built with Strict Ethical & Privacy Constraints

When victims are under stress, security matters most. Saathi AI enforces strict principles to guarantee that sensitive credentials remain completely protected:

  • ✓
    Never Solicits or Stores Passwords/PINs: Saathi AI will NEVER ask for your OTP, MPIN, ATM PIN, or CVV. If mentioned accidentally in voice notes, it is actively scrubbed.
  • ✓
    Ephemeral Audio Spooling: Audio recordings are unlinked and permanently deleted from disk immediately after transcription.
  • ✓
    Transparent Simulation Labeling: Any mock features (such as prototype FIR simulations) are prominently watermarked as Demo mode to avoid misrepresentation.
