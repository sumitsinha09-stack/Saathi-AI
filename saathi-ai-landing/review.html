Call National Cybercrime Helpline 1930

        
      

      

        

          1930 is India's official National Cyber Financial Fraud Helpline operated by the Ministry of Home Affairs (I4C).
        

        

          
    
            
  • Immediate Golden Hour: Reporting within 2–3 hours of unauthorized debit maximizes the chance of freezing funds in transit.
    • 
            
  • Notify Bank Promptly: Always contact your bank's fraud support alongside calling 1930.
    • 
            
  • Preserve Evidence: Keep SMS alerts, UPI transaction reference numbers, screenshots, and bank statements ready.
    • 
            
  • Official Portal: You should also file a formal digital complaint on cybercrime.gov.in when feasible.
    • 
          

        

        

          ℹ️ Desktop Note: If your browser or computer cannot place calls directly, please dial 1930 manually from your mobile telephone.

How to File Your Complaint on the Official Cybercrime Portal

        
      

      

        

          

            Official Government Portal:
            
              https://cybercrime.gov.in/ ↗
            
          

          ⚠️ Always verify the address bar displays .gov.in before entering personal credentials.
        


        
7-Step Official Filing Guide:

        
    
          
  1. Open the official portal: Go to cybercrime.gov.in in a secure browser tab.
    2. 
          
  2. Select the appropriate category: Choose "Report Financial Fraud" (Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System).
    3. 
          
  3. Registration / Authentication: Login with your name and mobile number via SMS OTP verification.
    4. 
          
  4. Enter Incident Information: Enter the incident date, time, and incident category matching your Saathi AI verified summary.
    5. 
          
  5. Enter Transaction Details: Fill in the beneficiary account / UPI handle, amount, and bank name.
    6. 
          
  6. Upload Supporting Evidence: Attach bank statements, screenshots, and call logs as evidence.
    7. 
          
  7. Submit and Save Acknowledgement: Review all entered data carefully, submit, and record your official government acknowledgement number.
    8. 
        


        

          ℹ️ Reference Number Distinction: Your Saathi AI case reference (SAI-XXXXXX) is for your internal relief tracking. The government portal will generate its own distinct official complaint acknowledgement number.
